# Final Goal

The goal isn't:

> "I know English grammar."

The goal is:

```text
Understand English
       ↓
Read English
       ↓
Listen to English
       ↓
Think in English
       ↓
Speak English
       ↓
Explain ideas in English
       ↓
Write clearly
       ↓
Communicate professionally
       ↓
Communicate naturally
```

Eventually, you should be able to learn anything from English resources without English itself becoming a barrier.

For a software-engineering journey, the highest-priority areas are:

**Grammar → Vocabulary → Listening → Speaking → Technical English → Professional Communication → Writing.**
