# Final Goal

Don't aim to simply memorize formulas.

The real goal is:

```text
Understand the concept
        ↓
Understand why it works
        ↓
Understand the formula
        ↓
Solve simple problems
        ↓
Solve unfamiliar problems
        ↓
Apply it to programming
        ↓
Apply it to real-world problems
        ↓
Explain it to someone else
        ↓
Prove it when possible
```

For a technical career, the highest-priority mathematical areas after the basics are:

**Algebra → Functions → Logarithms → Probability → Statistics → Discrete Math → Graph Theory → Combinatorics → Number Theory → Linear Algebra → Calculus.**

You do not need to become a mathematician before becoming a strong software engineer. The objective is to build enough mathematical intuition that **DSA, databases, system design, cryptography, graphics, algorithms, and eventually AI/ML stop feeling mathematically intimidating.**
